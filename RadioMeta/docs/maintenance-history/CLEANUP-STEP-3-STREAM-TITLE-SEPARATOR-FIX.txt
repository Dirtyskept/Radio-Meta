RADIO META v1.3.107 — ARTIST/TITLE DASH-SEPARATOR HANDLING
=========================================================

Why this change
---------------
Some station metadata separates artist and title with an en dash (–) or em dash (—)
instead of the ordinary hyphen-minus ( - ). The previous parser split only on the
ordinary hyphen separator, so a title such as "Rita Wilson – Everybody Cries" could
be accepted as a whole song title with no artist.

What changed
------------
The shared stream-title parser now recognizes these spaced separators:
  Artist - Song
  Artist – Song
  Artist — Song

It selects the earliest recognized separator and preserves the remainder of the song
title, including any later hyphens or dashes. Titles without a recognized separator
continue to be retained as a song title with an empty artist.

What did not change
-------------------
- No ICY/Shoutcast timeout or interval limit changed.
- No metadata request, cache, StationOnly, StationTransition, or publication policy
  was changed.
- No proxy, station file, library refresh, security, or WebLoader behavior changed.
- No minification was performed. Readable source remains in the package.
- The plugin GUID and configuration XML identity are unchanged.

Suggested test
--------------
Build on the Windows Emby server and test a metadata-producing station that sends an
en-dash or em-dash between artist and title (Affinity Radio is a useful candidate).
Verify that the artist and song split correctly. Also test an ordinary "Artist - Song"
station and a title with an additional dash later in the song title. Then verify station
switching and OZFM's StationOnly behavior remain correct.

Verification limitation
----------------------
Dashboard JavaScript syntax, source/bundle consistency, and package integrity can be
checked statically. The C# project was not compiled here because the .NET SDK and the
user's Emby build assemblies are unavailable in this environment.
