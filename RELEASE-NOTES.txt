Radio Meta v1.3.157 Mainline Source

Repeated artist removed from song titles. Some stations send the artist twice,
e.g. "Ella Langley - Ella Langley - Choosin' Texas", which showed as the song
"Ella Langley - Choosin' Texas". When a song title starts with exactly the
artist's name followed by a dash, that part is now removed, so it shows as
"Choosin' Texas". This applies to new songs everywhere Radio Meta shows them;
History entries already saved are not changed. Titles that only contain the
name, such as "Killer Queen" by Queen, are left alone, and so are songs named
after their artist with a version after the dash ("Black Sabbath - 2009
Remaster" by Black Sabbath). A title correction saved from History before this
version, for a title that had the artist repeated, may need saving again.
Everything in v1.3.156 is retained:

LIVE lines in theme colours. The LIVE line and the word now use the theme
colour of the bar they stand in for, and follow the theme when it changes:
in the big player and mini-player the colour of the played part of Emby's
slider (white in the dark themes); on the Dashboard card Emby's progress bar
colour (the accent colour, green by default). The big player's line is as thick
as Emby's slider, the Dashboard line as thick as Emby's progress bar; the
mini-player keeps its 3 px line. Everything in v1.3.155 is retained:

LIVE line on the Dashboard. On the Dashboard's Now Playing card, a device
playing a Radio Meta station now shows the same LIVE line as the mini-player,
at exactly the same size, where Emby draws a song's progress bar. The card
keeps the same height as a song card. It shows on every device's card where
Radio Meta shows the station's song, EmbyAudio included. Everything in v1.3.154 is retained:

LIVE line in Emby's web players. While a live station plays (audio with no
running time), the progress slider is replaced by a thin line with "LIVE" in
the middle, as in the EmbyAudio app: rounded lines in the text colour at 25%,
the word at 60%, semi-bold. Mini-player: along its top line (3 px line, 10 px
text), with its elapsed time hidden. Big player: in the slider's place (4 px
line, 13 px text); the time numbers are hidden but keep their space, so
nothing moves. Songs and other items with a running time keep Emby's slider,
and it comes back as soon as one starts or playback stops. The lock screen, CarPlay, seeking,
History and the settings page are unchanged. Everything in v1.3.153 is
retained.
